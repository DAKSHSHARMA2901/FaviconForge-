📚 Instructions:

  1. Upload your logo image - Drag & drop or click to select
  2. Customize your favicon - Adjust colors, padding, brightness, contrast, rotation
  3. Preview results - See how it looks in browsers and devices
  4. Download files - Get individual sizes, ZIP archive, or ICO format
  5. Copy HTML tags - Paste into your website's <head> section
  6. Generate manifest - Create web app manifest for PWA support
  7. Upload to your website - Place files in your public/root directory
💡 Pro Tips:
  • Use high-resolution source images (at least 512x512) for best results
  • Simple logos work better than complex designs for small favicon sizes
  • Test transparency option to see how your logo looks on different backgrounds
  • Use the preview section to ensure your favicon is clearly visible
